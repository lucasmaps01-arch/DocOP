How to prepare a document for DocOP

Use a text-based PDF or DOCX—not a scan, photo, or handwritten page. Keep one topic per section, use clear headings, and add a heading named Learning Objectives or Objectives with bullet points. Avoid tables, multi-column layouts, and password protection.

If a PDF is not searchable or is poorly structured, format it with an AI tool first. Review it for accuracy, remove confidential patient data, then save it as a DOCX or searchable PDF before uploading.

Convert this study document into a clean, accurate, study-ready PDF for an exam-practice app. Preserve every fact and do not add new information. Use one clear topic heading per section, normal paragraphs and simple bullet lists only. Remove repeated headers and footers, page numbers, decorative formatting, tables, columns, and images. Create a section headed Learning Objectives with concise bullet points only when objectives are explicitly present in the source. Keep clinical terms, numbers, doses, warnings, and references exactly as written. Return the result as a downloadable, searchable PDF.

Terms & Conditions

DocOP is an educational study tool for document review, revision questions, and simulated OSCE practice. It is not a medical device, healthcare provider, diagnostic service, or substitute for professional judgement, supervision, diagnosis, or treatment.

Use of content

You are responsible for ensuring that material you upload is accurate, lawful to use, and free of unnecessary personal or patient-identifying information. Always verify educational material against current trusted sources and local clinical guidance.

AI-generated material

Generated questions, explanations, simulations, and feedback may be incomplete or incorrect. They are for learning support only and must not be used as patient-specific advice or as the sole basis for clinical decisions.

Privacy and storage

Uploaded documents and saved learning progress are stored locally by the app unless a feature clearly states otherwise. Do not upload confidential patient records or sensitive personal data.
